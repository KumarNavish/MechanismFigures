## How the selection was made

Candidates were compared for a tangible scientific object, a traceable operation, a visible invariant or intervention, and a composition that exposes a relationship rather than merely naming stages. Full paper/figure context and actual rendered images were inspected. Several attractive candidates were rejected for generic module diagrams, result-only curves, decorative effects, or redundancy. The complete decision ledger, retained hashes and rejected-candidate reasons are in [curation-2026-10-08.json](curation-2026-10-08.json).

This is reasoned visual curation, not a controlled preference study or proof that these are the universally best references. Source figures retain their own rights and limitations. The supplied 4D-printing, layered photonics, protein denoising, tissue packing, inverse optics, relaxation-driven machines, spatial layer decomposition and mechanism-synthesis examples teach different operations; they should not become a single decorative style.

## How the selection was made

Candidates were compared for a tangible scientific object, a traceable operation, a visible invariant or intervention, and a composition that exposes a relationship rather than merely naming stages. Full paper/figure context and actual rendered images were inspected. Several attractive candidates were rejected for generic module diagrams, result-only curves, decorative effects, or redundancy. The complete decision ledger, retained hashes and rejected-candidate reasons are in [curation-2026-10-08.json](curation-2026-10-08.json).

This is reasoned visual curation, not a controlled preference study or proof that these are the universally best references. Source figures retain their own rights and limitations. The supplied 4D-printing, layered photonics, protein denoising, tissue packing, inverse optics, relaxation-driven machines, spatial layer decomposition and mechanism-synthesis examples teach different operations; they should not become a single decorative style.

# Curation revision — 13 November 2026

The user asked for a larger atlas with a strong bias for 3D. This revision keeps all 20 references of the October canon with the same image bytes, puts the 3D references first, and adds 22 references.

**The 22 additions are curator selections. The user has not reviewed them.** Each entry records this in its `approval` field.

How they were selected:

- Four searches by field (molecular machines; physics and materials; machine learning, vision and neuroscience; earth science and development) gave 48 candidates. About 190 other figures were viewed and rejected in those searches.
- Each candidate had to show an operation and its consequence in a 3D construction, at a readable size.
- Each of the 22 has a CC BY 4.0 licence. The licence was read from the article page and checked against the Crossref record or the arXiv abstract page. Figure captions were not checked for third-party exceptions.
- Each image was opened and read before its entry was written.
- The 26 candidates that were not selected were dense, small, low in resolution, or showed a structure with no operation.

Euclidean projection onto the halfspace x ≥ 0. The dashed input vector (−1.8, 1.2) is mapped to (0, 1.2); the horizontal displacement removes the infeasible normal component and preserves the tangential coordinate. Equal axis scales are used, and all coordinates are dimensionless. This is an exact analytical toy example, not a measured optimization trajectory, a new algorithm, or a claim about convergence or runtime. Data and checks are generated by render.py.

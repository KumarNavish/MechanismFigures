## Mechanism

Unspliced and spliced RNA respond with a lag as a gene switches on and off.

## Where to look

1. Read the transcription–splicing–degradation chain.
2. Trace the induction and repression arms in unspliced–spliced coordinates.
3. Notice that equal spliced abundance need not imply the same direction of change.

## Why it works

A molecular processing chain sits above the joint phase portrait. Oriented trajectory arms and time/state color separate induction from repression.

## Take this

When two quantities lag each other, pair them as coordinates; check whether direction or hysteresis becomes visible.

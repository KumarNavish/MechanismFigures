## Mechanism

Updating a residue-pair representation incorporates information involving a third residue.

## Where to look

1. In panel b, match selected matrix entries to edges between residues i, j and k.
2. In panel c, follow the blue edges through the third residue.
3. In the full figure, relate these local updates to the structure module and its explicit residue frames.

## Why it works

The same indices label matrix cells, graph edges and triangle operations. The surrounding architecture stays quieter than the selected local update.

## Take this

When indices hide relational structure, draw the objects they refer to and preserve the direction of each dependency.

## Test your figure

Can every highlighted edge be matched to a term in the update? Do not imply physical transport or causation when it is only computation.

## Limit

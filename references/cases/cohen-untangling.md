## Mechanism

All images of one object make a manifold in the space of neural responses. Early in the network, the manifolds of two objects are folded into each other. Later stages make them smaller and move them apart until a plane separates them.

## Where to look

1. Left: the blue sheet and the red sheet are folded into each other. Each dot is one image.
2. Middle: after one stage, the sheets are less folded.
3. Right: the sheets are small and far apart, and a plane between them separates the two classes.

## Why it works

Three states in one camera with fixed colours. The object that changes is the shape of the sheets, and nothing else moves. Example images hang from points on the sheets, so the abstract sheet has a concrete meaning.

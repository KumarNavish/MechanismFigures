**The same sheet grows into different shapes when the polarity field is different.**

## Mechanism

A tissue is a sheet with a growth rate along and across a local polarity direction. Different patterns of rate and polarity make a cone, a dome or a spike from the same flat square.

## Where to look

1. Read one row from left to right: a flat sheet grows to 2.3 times and to 5 times its area.
2. The arrows on the sheet are the polarity. The colour is the growth rate.
3. Compare the rows. Cases J, K, L and M start as squares and end as different shapes.

## Why it works

A grid of small multiples: time goes across and the condition goes down. One camera. The mesh lines show the deformation. The causes, polarity and rate, are painted on the surface that they shape.

## Take this

Put time on one axis of a grid and the condition on the other. Paint the cause on the object. Keep a mesh so that the deformation is visible.

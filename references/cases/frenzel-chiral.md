## Mechanism

A transverse wave moves through a lattice of chiral unit cells. The direction of the displacement rotates round the direction of travel.

## Where to look

1. Panel a: one column of the lattice has colour. The colour is the phase of the motion, and it changes along the column.
2. Panel b: the same column as an abstraction. The red displacement arrows rotate round the axis k.
3. Panel c: the unit cell that causes the rotation, with its dimensions.

## Why it works

Three views of one thing: the real structure, the abstraction and the cause. The context lattice is pale and one column carries colour. The abstraction in panel b keeps the orientation of panel a, so the eye maps one to the other.

## Take this

Show the real system with one element in colour. Then draw that element alone as a clean abstraction in the same orientation. Then show the unit that causes the behaviour.

## Test your figure

Does the abstract panel have the same orientation and the same colours as the element in the real view?

## Limit

The lattice behind the column is busy. The phase wheel is cyclic and is hard for some colour-blind readers.

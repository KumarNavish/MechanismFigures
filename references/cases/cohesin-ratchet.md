# Cohesin loop extrusion

**One gripping cycle moves the DNA a step and makes the loop larger.**

## Mechanism

Cohesin grips DNA, lets it slip, and uses random thermal motion in one direction only. After each cycle of grip and release, more DNA is in the loop.

## Where to look

1. Row B: follow the blue DNA through states a to f. The protein ring keeps its colours.
2. State c: the small double arrows mark the random motion that the ratchet uses.
3. State f: the loop is larger. The cyan mark on the DNA shows the movement of one cycle.

## Why it works

A cycle of six states with one camera. The DNA is a thick simple line and the protein is a detailed surface, so the two never merge. A mark on the DNA makes the net movement visible. Row A shows a different result from the same start.

## Mechanism

The sample tilts about two axes. Each tilt series gives phase images, and each set of images gives one component of the magnetic induction as a volume. The condition of zero divergence gives the third component.

## Where to look

1. In the centre, find the needle sample in the hole of the holder, and the phase image that it projects below.
2. At the left and the right, the holder tilts about x and about y. Each series gives one component, Bx or By, as a volume.
3. Follow the grey triangles to the equation. Zero divergence gives Bz, and panel b shows the full field.

## Why it works

The geometry of the measurement is the figure. Each data product sits below the physical setup that makes it, thus the layout is the pipeline. Colour identity holds: red is the x series and blue is the y series, in the axes, the labels and the equation.

## Take this

When a result comes from several measurements, draw each measurement as a physical scene and put its data directly below it. Join the data products at the equation that combines them.

## Test your figure

Can the reader say which measurement gives which component of the result?

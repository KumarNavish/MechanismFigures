## Mechanism

Sixteen stator units insert between the collar and the C-ring of the motor. Protons go through each stator, and the stator touches the FliG ring of the rotor, where the torque is made.

## Where to look

1. Compare the top row (no stator, ΔmotB) with the bottom row (wild type). The yellow stator units appear in the bottom row only.
2. Columns C and G: a cut through the motor. In G, follow the arrow of H+ through the stator.
3. Columns D and H: the C-ring from the side. In H, the stator touches the green ring.

## Why it works

Two rows and four cameras that are the same in each row. The intervention has one colour, yellow, and it is absent in the top row. A background tint names the condition of each row. The zoom goes from the whole, to the cut, to the contact.

## Take this

Put a mutant or an ablation in the top row and the full system in the bottom row. Keep the cameras the same. Give the added part one colour.

## Test your figure

Is the added part the only difference between the rows?

## Limit

The maps are densities from electron tomography of cells. The proton path is an interpretation.

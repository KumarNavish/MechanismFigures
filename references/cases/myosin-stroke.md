## Mechanism

The motor domain stays bound to actin while its lever arm swings from the primed position to the post-stroke position. The end of the lever moves about 34 nanometres.

## Where to look

1. Panel a: one motor on the grey actin filament with two lever positions, primed (dark blue) and post-stroke (light blue). The bar gives the distance.
2. Panels b and c: the same pair from the end and from above.
3. Panel d: two heads of one molecule, one in each state. The ends of their levers meet within 5 nanometres.

## Why it works

Two states are laid on one fixed body, so the movement is the angle between them. A scale bar makes the picture a measurement. The track is grey. Three orthogonal views with rotation marks remove the ambiguity of depth.

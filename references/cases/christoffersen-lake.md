## Mechanism

Water from the surface reaches the bed and the ice above speeds up. The stress at a lake changes from compression to tension. A crevasse opens, the lake drains, and the new water at the bed starts the same process at the next lake.

## Where to look

1. Panel a: a block of the ice sheet with a lake, a moulin and water at the bed. Red shows acceleration.
2. Panels b to d: the same block at later times. Follow the blue water as it reaches the bed at new places.
3. The small box below each block gives the sign of the stress at the lake.

## Why it works

The same block in four states with one camera. Two colours with fixed meanings: blue is water and red is faster flow. A small stress glyph below each state gives the cause, in the same position each time.

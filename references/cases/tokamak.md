## Mechanism

Rings of coils surround a vacuum vessel. Their currents shape the plasma in the vessel. The controller sets the coil voltages to hold the plasma boundary, the X-point and the legs at their targets.

## Where to look

1. Panel g: the nested rings are the coils. A wedge is cut away, and the plasma shows in the two cut faces.
2. Follow the dashed lines from the right cut face to panel h.
3. Panel h: the flat cross-section with the plasma boundary, the X-point and the legs. These are the control targets.

## Why it works

The cutaway shows where things are. The flat section shows what to measure. Dashed lines join one cut face to the flat panel, so the reader knows that the two are the same plane.

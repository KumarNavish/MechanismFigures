## Mechanism

The injectisome moves an unfolded protein from the base, through the export apparatus and up the channel of the needle, into the host cell.

## Where to look

1. Panel a, left: the complete needle complex. Right: the same view, cut in half.
2. In the cut view, follow the magenta substrate from the base up the channel.
3. Panels b and c: the views from the two ends, and the inner parts alone with their names.

## Why it works

The whole body and the cut body stand adjacent in the same camera and scale. One saturated colour, magenta, marks the thing that moves through. A small schematic at the top left places the machine in the cell.

## Mechanism

Each wedge of the ellipsoid body sends neurons to one glomerulus in each half of the protocerebral bridge. One bump of activity on the ring thus becomes two bumps in the bridge.

## Where to look

1. Panel Aii: the reconstructed neurons. The colour of each neuron is the ring wedge that it comes from.
2. Panel B: the same colours on the ring diagram and on the row of bridge glomeruli. Follow the two purple arrows from one wedge.
3. Bottom: one activity bump on the ring. It becomes one bump in each half of the bridge.

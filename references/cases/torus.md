## Mechanism

Grid-cell population activity has two periodic coordinates; physical positions can revisit the same population phase.

## Where to look

1. In panel a, follow how opposite edges of the unwrapped domain correspond on the torus.
2. In b, compare repeated firing fields in physical space with compact toroidal hotspots.
3. The same activity is organized differently because the coordinates identify periodic states.

## Why it works

The torus and its unwrapped domain make periodic identifications visible, beside physical and toroidal activity maps.

## Take this

If repetitions obscure a rule, factor out the repetition—but show exactly which positions are identified.

## Test your figure

Does the coordinate change preserve the relation claimed? Support topology with analysis, not the appearance of a projection.

## Do not copy

A torus-shaped rendering does not establish toroidal topology.

## Limit

This is a topology of population activity, not an anatomical doughnut. It does not by itself prove a specific biophysical circuit.

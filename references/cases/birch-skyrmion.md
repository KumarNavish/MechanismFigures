## Mechanism

A skyrmion is a tube through the thickness of the sample. A tube can end inside the sample at a singular point, a Bloch point, and the point moves when the tube changes length.

## Where to look

1. Find the two layers of arrows. They are the top and the bottom surface of the sample.
2. Follow the three grey tubes between the layers. One tube does not reach the bottom.
3. The green dots are Bloch points. The inset shows one point as it moves when a tube gets shorter.

## Why it works

Two slices of data bound a ghost volume, so the eye builds the 3D structure between them. The exception, the short tube, carries the message. One inset shows the one part that moves.

## Take this

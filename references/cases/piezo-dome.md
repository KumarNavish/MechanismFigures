# Piezo membrane footprint

**The protein curves the membrane into a dome, and the dome deforms the membrane far beyond it.**

## Mechanism

The Piezo channel holds the membrane in a dome. The membrane round the dome must bend to join the flat membrane far away. This large footprint stores energy, and membrane tension can flatten it.

## Where to look

1. Panels A and B: the three blades of the protein lie on a spherical cap, from the side and from the top.
2. Panel C: the grey cap is that dome, at the centre of a much larger surface.
3. The blue surface is the membrane footprint. A wedge is cut away to show the profile.

## Why it works

A pull-back in scale: first the molecule, then the molecule as a small cap in a large deformed sheet. A mesh on the surface makes the curvature readable. One wedge cut shows the profile.

## Take this

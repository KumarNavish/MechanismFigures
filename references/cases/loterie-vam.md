## Mechanism

A modulator sends a different light pattern into a rotating vial of resin for each angle. The dose adds up in the volume and the part becomes solid. A second beam at 90 degrees observes the part.

## Where to look

1. Start at the DLP modulator at the left. Follow the blue beam through the two lenses to the vial.
2. Look at the strip of projections below the modulator: one pattern for each angle of the vial.
3. Find the part inside the yellow resin. Then follow the red beam to the camera, which observes the part from the side.

## Why it works

One optical axis runs across the page, and each element sits on it in its real sequence. The beams are translucent volumes, so the eye sees where the light is. Two colours separate two jobs: blue writes and red observes. The container is a ghost, so the part inside it stays visible.

## Take this

Put the full apparatus on one axis in one camera. Draw the thing that travels (light, fluid, signal) as a translucent volume with its own colour. Use a second colour for a second path that has a different job.

## Test your figure

Can a reader point to where the energy enters, where it acts and where it is measured?

## Limit

The figure shows the apparatus. The accumulation of dose, which is the core of the method, is implied by the projection strip and is not drawn.

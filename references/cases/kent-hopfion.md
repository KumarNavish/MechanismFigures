# Hopfion in a multilayer

## Mechanism

A target skyrmion has the same spin pattern at each height. A hopfion has a ring of twisted spins in the middle layers only. A surface-sensitive X-ray method and a bulk method thus give different images for the hopfion.

## Where to look

1. Top cylinder, target skyrmion: on the two cut faces, the arrow pattern is the same at each height.
2. Bottom cylinder, hopfion: on the cut faces, the arrows turn round a ring in the middle and stay vertical at the top and the bottom.
3. At the right, compare the two predicted X-ray images for each texture. The two methods differ for the hopfion only.

## Why it works

The same quarter cut and the same camera for the two states make the texture the only thing that changes. The cut faces carry the field, so the inside becomes readable. The layer stack at the left ties each texture to the structure that hosts it.

## Take this

To compare two internal states, cut the two bodies with the same planes and view them with the same camera. Put the field on the cut faces.

## Test your figure

Does the difference between the states show on the cut faces with no caption?

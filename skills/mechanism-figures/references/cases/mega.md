# MEGA-plate evolution: Place history inside its environment

Baym et al. · Science 2016 · Figure 1B

[Publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5534434/) · [Original asset](https://www.molecularecologist.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/MEGA2.jpg)

**Use when:** Branching, adaptation or movement depends on spatially organized obstacles or resources.

## Look in this order

1. Read the antibiotic bands from the outside toward the center.
2. Follow a lineage as the colony expands and branches across those bands.
3. Keep the ancestry overlay and the photographed growth landscape in view together.

## Mechanism → construction → immediate insight

**Mechanism:** Adaptation unfolds while expanding populations encounter successively stronger antibiotic barriers.

**Construction:** Place the ancestry tree directly on the physical growth landscape, with resistance measurements and concentration bands.

**The eye sees:** Evolution has a route through space: branching and advancement occur in a structured environment, not in an abstract tree.

**Why not an ordinary plot:** A resistance-versus-time curve removes the geography. A detached tree removes the selective obstacles.

## Recreate the explanatory operation

**Replace the objects:** Plate → your environment; concentration band → barrier; lineage → inferred history; colony image → observed state.

**Preserve:** Do not detach the path from its physical coordinates. Mark inferred ancestry separately from direct observation.

1. Use the environment as the base layer.
2. Place events and trajectories at their actual locations and stages.
3. Attach the relevant local measurements without replacing the environmental context.

**Acceptance test:** Can the reader explain where progression stalled and where it advanced? Validate event ordering and ancestry separately.

**Do not copy literally:** A shortest path drawn after the fact is not an observed history.

**Transfer example (proposal, not a finding):** For adaptive exploration, overlay the verified search trajectory and branch points on the actual obstacle or reward landscape.

**Scientific boundary:** The photograph is observed; ancestry connections are inferred from video. Resistance measurements and environmental concentration are distinct quantities.
